# How to submit a project or bounty to the Litecoin Foundation

Submit a project or a bounty to the Litecoin Foundation at https://litecoin.com/projects/submit. That form is the submission path. A bounty is submitted the same way as any other project: it becomes a listing on the Foundation's projects directory. There is no separate application site.

The form is for a project that can benefit the Litecoin community. After the Foundation approves a submission, the project is listed on litecoin.com, where people can donate to it. The Foundation reviews the information you send to see whether it aligns with its goals and values. If the project is selected, the Foundation follows up for anything still needed to finish the listing. That follow-up can include a Litecoin address or other payment details so donations can be paid out.

Approved work appears on the projects directory at https://litecoin.com/projects. That directory lists open-source efforts such as Litecoin Core, MWEB, and Litecoin Space, and it lists bounties such as the Litecoin Core bug bounty and completed ports, including Ordinals Lite, Litecoin Space, and Litescribe. The directory's own call to action is the same form. The Foundation says it is looking for people and teams working on decentralized open-source Litecoin software.

## What the form asks for

The form collects a project name and description, a main focus, the potential impact, a repository link if you have one, and social or contact links. The focus list includes Litecoin Core, meta protocols such as Ordinals and Omni, Ordinals Lite, the Litecoin Dev Kit, LTC Suite, Lightning, atomic swaps, education and guides, wallets, and other. It asks whether the project is open source: yes, no, or partially. It asks for a proposed budget and whether the project has received funding before. It also asks for your name, email, whether you are the project lead, a personal GitHub or similar profile if you have one, other contact details, prior contributions, and references.

## Donation terms on a listing

Donations to a listed project go through the Foundation. The submit page states two terms. A 15% service fee is applied to each donation to cover operational costs, including administration and marketing. The Litecoin Foundation's Open Source Fund Council tries to honor a donor's intent, and it reserves the right to move funds between supported projects when priorities change.

Sending the form does not grant LTC, does not promise that a submission will be listed, and does not replace a press, partnership, or scam-report contact. For a project or bounty listing, use https://litecoin.com/projects/submit.
