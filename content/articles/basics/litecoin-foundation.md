# What is the Litecoin Foundation?

The Litecoin Foundation is a non-profit organization that supports Litecoin. It funds development, education, and public resources, and it publishes project listings and learning material on litecoin.com. It is not Litecoin itself. The protocol runs on the computers of miners and full-node operators. The Foundation does not issue LTC, does not set the block reward, and does not custody coins for users.

A gift to the Foundation, or a membership, is support for that organization's work. It is not a purchase of LTC and it does not give anyone control of the chain. The Foundation can sponsor software, bounties, and events. It cannot reverse a transaction, freeze an address, or change consensus rules on its own. Rule changes ship only when the people running the network adopt them.

The Foundation's public face includes the litecoin.com site, the projects directory (Litecoin Core, Litecoin Space, wallets, and other ecosystem work), and outreach to developers, merchants, and new users. Staff and volunteers speak for the organization only when they are doing that job. A chat answer generated from published articles is not a statement of the Foundation, and this article does not list current officers, budgets, or legal filings.

If you need a comment for the press, a partnership, or a scam report, use the contact paths the Foundation publishes. Do not treat a general description of the organization as a live reply from its staff.
